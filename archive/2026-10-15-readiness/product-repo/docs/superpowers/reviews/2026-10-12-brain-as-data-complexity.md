# Brain-as-data branch — complexity review (2026-10-12)

Read-only. Nine reviewers, one per part, each asked: "I feel like there is something fundamentally wrong, find the main
source of complexity." Each names ONE main source with evidence, and at most three secondary ones. Simplify nothing —
the DECISION column is the user's.

## Decision table

| # | Part | Source of complexity | Simpler shape | Cost to change | What it loses | DECISION |
|---|------|----------------------|---------------|----------------|---------------|----------|
| 1 | Platform/Parts — part log, leases, offline journal | One log with **two durability semantics selected by machine role**: on an Own machine `Append` acks after local journal fsync (`PartLog.cs:174-193`), on others after the head CAS; ~18 sites branch on the journal regime (`_committedUnder`, `HeadPayload.Writers`/`SeqWriter`, `Recover`, two `Apply` sites, role-based error policy). | One durability contract (apply/ack on commit); demote the journal to a write-behind cache that never affects ack, `_records`, or reopen validation. | High — `Recover`, `_committedUnder`, `Writers`/`SeqWriter`, both `Apply` sites and the role branches unwind; `PartJournal` shrinks away. | Q26: an Own machine accepting writes through a sequencer outage at local-fsync latency (a real requirement). |
| 2 | Platform/Mesh + Trust/Mesh — storage, audits, credits | A shard's **placement fact has no single home**: minted in `StorageNodeDirectoryGrain.Issue` (`:214-248`), sealed per shard in the manifest (`MeshStore.cs:156`), echoed in `AuditReport.PlacementId`, re-derived by a four-way join in `Report` (`:168-178`); two independent eviction policies (`PlacedFor` 64/node vs `PlacementsOf` 1024/account). | One `Placement` record keyed by id `(accounts, node, shardIndex, cid, issuedAt)`; manifest references it; delete `PlacedFor`, derive and collapse the two caps into one expiry. | ~1 day — reshape `StorageNodeDirectoryState` (no migration needed), update `Issue`/`Report`/`Keep` + credit facts. | R117's node-pressure signal (64 accounts/node, oldest evicted) — must be re-expressed as placement expiry. |
| 3 | Platform/Keys — custody, part keys, agreement keys, Windows TPM | The part-key invariant **split in two by `BrainKind`**: owner parts use `PartKeys` (master-key wrap), circle parts use `CirclePartKeys` (per-machine ECDH wraps); two contracts (`IPartKeys`/`ICirclePartKeys`), one grain fusing both state shapes, `KindOf` re-branched at ~15 sites; confinement is crypto in one case and policy-only in the other. | One `IPartKeyring.KeysOf(PartId)` → `machineId → wrappedKey` from one authority (the directory's machine list), one wrap format; an owner brain is the 1-authority case. | Mechanical + an authority move (owner authority from open-time to wrap-time); storage wipe is fine. | Nothing structural; the pre-owner-record "any own machine may open" window must become an explicit machine set. |

| 6 | Modules/Circles + `features/os` circle apps | **A circle is a brain AND a neuron that re-materializes the brain's identity**: owner/members/machines live in `BrainAuthorityState` and again in `CircleRecord` (`CircleNeuron.cs:12-32`), hand-synchronized in ~13 methods (note the `Union(...MachinesOf)` reconciliations at `:152,189`), and re-checked at three authorization layers. | One owner of membership (the authority, or the neuron), the other holds only circle-specific state; mutation becomes a single write. | High — `MachinesOf` is read by lease preference, routing, forwarded standing, key rotation; membership by grants/chat/factory. | The in-grain membership check's locality (no remote round-trip) — in tension with "state lives in the neuron". |

### Secondary sources (per part, one line each)

- **1 P arts:** six parallel in-memory projections (`_records/_since/_chain/_chunks/_committedUnder/_replayed`); snapshot as a dual inline/chunked representation; a grain's "stand-in" meaning is a mutable flag set as a side effect of a storage read (`PartGrainStorage.cs:17-24` → `PartRouter._standIns`).
- **2 Mesh:** the storage node is a sum type encoded as nullable fields + a `node/` string prefix, with duplicated claim-arbitration; the audit round straddles mutable head and immutable manifest (`HeadPayload.AuditRound` reconstructed in four sites); credits exist twice, reconciled by a best-effort pull (sound — leave it).
- **3 Keys:** six hand-rolled seal/wrap formats for one primitive, with the same configured secret backing two different master-key derivations; `IMasterKeyCustody` claims account-scope but TPM custody is machine-scope; `SealedSecret` string-prefix seal-scheme discriminant; `Shamir` has no production caller.
- **4 Trust:** machine↔account binding stored in two grains; `BrainKind.Circle` special-cased through every gate; the `Authorize`/`Consume` split.
- **5 Links:** the trust link is a hand-rolled RPC with a 3-place registry; NAT traversal is three mechanisms (punch/rendezvous/relay) where relay-only would do; caller trust re-derived at the holder with hidden 30 s caches.
- **6 Circles:** compute share is a third copy of owner+members (`CircleShareGrain`); a machine is a triple (record + authority + keyring wraps); a shared app is four artifacts.
- **7 Kernel:** `FeatureLoader` bypassed and app existence inferred from a manifest field; `BrainKind` re-queried (~10 sites) and encoded twice (id prefix); one `Notify` drives three live-delivery registries.
- **8 Plan 9:** redundant verification/anti-collusion; payment by fuel through the ledger; the private/not-private compute gate.
- **9 Flame:** whole-rebuild in C# re-diffed at five independent Dart layers; the stage has two identities/owners (`OwnerKeyOf` vs `ScopeOf`); optimistic concurrency hand-rolled at every call site.

### Honest notes carried from the reviewers

- Parts (1), Mesh (2), Trust (4), Links (5), Circles (6), kernel (7) and Flame (9) are *deliberate* designs, not sloppiness; the complexity is the price of Q26 offline durability, of a separate trust cluster, of NAT-facing machines, of "state lives in the neuron", and of a data-scene/Dart-interpreter UI base. The reviewers name where the price is highest, not that it was unthinking.
- Plan 9 (8) and the part-sealed write path aside, no reviewer found the branch unsound; the mesh credit pull/ack was explicitly judged sound.
