# Maintaining the product handbook

Keep current product requirements, status, decisions and plans in this documentation repository. Do not recreate narrative plans in the product repository.

For each status change, record the exact source revision, the user outcome, and the evidence class. Separate proposed behavior from implemented paths and from observed acceptance. Historical evidence must keep its original date and environment.

When a contract changes, reconcile the handbook, generator prompts, examples, installed app behavior and UI expectations. A documentation update alone cannot fix a stale runtime prompt.

The product repository retains agent instructions, executable prompts, tooling/schema files and required packaging inputs. Those are operational inputs, not competing product handbooks.

The archived handbook includes pre-existing uncommitted edits. It is preserved for recovery and historical context, excluded from the active site. No commits, pushes or publication were performed by this assessment.
